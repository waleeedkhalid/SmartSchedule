✅ VALIDATION HIGHLIGHTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Preferences Submission:
  • Minimum 3 preferences
  • Maximum 10 preferences
  • All courses must be valid electives
  • No duplicate courses
  • Course codes format validation
  • Student must be ACTIVE status
  • Prevents re-submission

Schedule Access:
  • Only published schedules visible
  • Cannot view other students' schedules
  • Read-only access (cannot modify)

Feedback Submission:
  • Rating: 1-5 scale (required)
  • Comment max length: 1000 characters
  • Schedule must be published
  • One feedback per schedule
